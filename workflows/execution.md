# Execution

Convert a clear intention into three things:

- Target: what will change.
- Completion check: how you will know.
- First action: what you can do now.

When stuck, identify the cause before responding. Missing information needs a focused answer. A technical defect needs reproduction and debugging. A large task needs a smaller slice. An unclear step needs a concrete action. Low energy or resistance needs a smaller demand or chosen rest. Evidence of avoidance needs a calm challenge and immediate restart.

If no new decision remains, stop comparing approaches.

After completion, check evidence. Record what changed and what the user learned or judged. Set the next project action. When skill formation is the purpose, preserve a small user attempt rather than completing the entire exercise for them.

Report once: the conversation or Daily Personal Notes. A checkbox is not a substitute for the result.

中文：目标明确时少分析、多启动；卡住时解决真正原因。完成后核对结果、记录判断、更新下一动作。
